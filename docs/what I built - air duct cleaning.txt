What I picked and why

HVAC Contractor's template had exactly one product — HVAC Service — and it's a thorough one, already covering install/replace/repair for AC, furnaces, heat pumps, mini-splits, and combo systems. So I looked for something genuinely separate rather than overlapping it, and picked Air Duct Cleaning — a standard, commonly-quoted HVAC service that has nothing to do with equipment installation or repair, so there's zero overlap with what's already there.

What I built

3 rules: a -10% bundle discount for 3+ systems, a $50 flat assessment fee if contamination is reported, and a +15% rush fee for same-day service.
